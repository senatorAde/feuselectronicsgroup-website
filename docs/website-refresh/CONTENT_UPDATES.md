# FEUS Website Refresh: Content Updates

Date: 2026-09-06

## Messaging Direction

The refreshed narrative separates the company from the product while showing how they reinforce one another:

- FEUS Electronics Group provides strategy, engineering, transformation, digital, media, and operational services.
- FEUS.ai is the governance-first AI Data Operations platform developed by FEUS Electronics Group.
- Human intent, target context, policy, identity, approval, and evidence remain visible parts of the operating story.
- Capability maturity and availability are qualified rather than generalized.

The lead positioning is: "Enterprise technology. Governed intelligence."

## Page-Level Changes

### Homepage

- New headline: "FEUS builds technology that moves business forward with control."
- Adds four trust principles: human-controlled, governance-first, status-transparent, and practitioner-led.
- Introduces six capability paths, four audience situations, four qualitative business outcomes, and a four-step delivery process.
- Gives consultation, solution exploration, Trust Center, FEUS.ai, and written-brief actions distinct labels.

### About

- New headline: "Technology should create momentum. Control should make it durable."
- Clarifies the relationship between FEUS Electronics Group and FEUS.ai.

### Services

## Claims and Evidence Rules

Content continues to derive platform posture from `src/data/publicStatus.js`. Exact-revision release evidence remains in Trust Center-only chunks.

The refresh intentionally does not introduce:

- customer names or logos;
- testimonials or review quotations;
- adoption, usage, or production-deployment counts;
- universal ROI, cost-saving, or speed-improvement percentages;
- blanket production-readiness or availability statements;
- new certifications, awards, partner badges, or service-level commitments.

Where visitors would normally expect proof, the site uses operating principles, scoped capability status, transparent limitations, engagement outputs, and qualitative outcome categories until approved evidence is available.
